I am a Ph.D. student at the University at Buffalo, advised by [Dr. Rui Zhang](https://ruizhang1121.github.io/index.html). My current research focuses on **near-field integrated sensing and communication (ISAC)** and **radio-over-fiber (RoF) communications**. Previously, I obtained my B.Eng. degree in Electronic Science and Technology from Xidian University.
